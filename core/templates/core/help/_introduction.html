Introduction

If you're anything like me, you may have a habit of quickly jotting things down in a notebook or on random pieces of paper so you can refer to them later. That works great -- until you end up with a small pile of notes on your desk and can't find the one you need, or worse, it gets thrown away before you've used the information you wrote down.

That used to happen to me a lot, and it's a big part of why RidgeNote was created.

RidgeNote is a self-hosted application built around a simple idea: your text-based notes should be easy to write, easy to organize, easy to find, and easy to recover. At its core, it was designed to be a better replacement for the everyday scratch pad.

It combines rich-text notes with folders, tags, search tools, Trash and recovery, and portable Library Backups in a compact browser-based interface.

RidgeNote is designed for individuals, households, and small trusted groups who want straightforward control over their own notes without the complexity of a full document-management or collaboration platform.

RidgeNote is not intended to replace an office suite, project-management system, or large collaborative document platform. Its focus is deliberately narrower: quickly creating and organizing notes, easily finding information again later, and protecting that information from accidental loss.

If you're new to RidgeNote, continue with Getting Started for a quick tour of the interface and the basics of working with your notes.
