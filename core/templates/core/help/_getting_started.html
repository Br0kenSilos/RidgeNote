Getting Started

After signing in, RidgeNote opens to Home, your starting point for navigating your library and getting back to the notes you use most often.

Home

Home gives you a quick view of your recent and pinned notes, along with a link to All Notes, where you can browse the complete list of notes in your library.

The folder tree

The folder tree appears on the left side of the screen, or behind the menu button on narrow screens. It lets you browse your folders and notes and provides quick access to creating and organizing them.

Notes can be placed inside a folder or left Unfiled if you don't need to organize them that way.

See Folders and Notes for more details.

Creating your first note

Select New note from the folder tree to create a note and start writing.

RidgeNote saves your work as you edit, so you can concentrate on the note instead of managing files or manually saving every change.

Once a note exists, you can rename it, move it to a folder, add tags, duplicate it, print or download it, and more. See Notes for the full set of note actions.

Finding things again

RidgeNote gives you several ways to get back to information you've already written:

  • Recent notes lets you quickly return to notes you've viewed recently.
  • Search in the top bar provides a fast way to find a note.
  • The folder-tree Filter helps narrow what you see while browsing.
  • Full Search provides more detailed searching and filtering across your entire library.

See Search for an explanation of when to use each one.

Account and Preferences

Open the account menu in the top-right corner to reach Account, Preferences, Tag manager, Library Backup, and other options available to your account.

Use Account for things such as your display name, email, password, and Library Backup tools. Use Preferences for settings such as your timezone, tag behavior, and appearance.

See Account and Preferences for details.
