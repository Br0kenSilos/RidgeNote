Account and Preferences

RidgeNote separates your account information from your personal application preferences.

Open the account menu in the top-right corner to reach Account, Preferences, and other options available to you.

Account

The Account page contains information and controls related to your RidgeNote identity and login.

From here you can:

  • Change your Display name, which is the name RidgeNote uses when identifying you throughout the interface.
  • Change your Email address.
  • View your Username, Role, and Last login information.
  • Use Change password to set a new password for your account.
  • Download a Library Backup or restore a previous one.

Your username is shown for reference but cannot currently be changed from the Account page.

See Library Backup and Restore for important information about backing up or replacing your note library.

Preferences

The Preferences page contains settings that control how RidgeNote behaves for you without changing your account identity.

Timezone

Your timezone determines how RidgeNote displays dates and times.

You can select a timezone manually or use Use browser timezone to detect the timezone reported by your browser.

This setting affects how timestamps are presented to you; it does not change the underlying time associated with your notes or other records.

Tag preferences

Preferences also includes controls for how new tags are created and named:

  • Uppercase tag names stores newly created or renamed tag names in uppercase.
  • Use semantic tag colors can automatically choose an appropriate color for certain recognized tag names.
  • Default color determines the starting color for new tags when another color has not been chosen.

Changing these settings does not automatically rewrite or recolor tags that already exist.

See Tags for more about creating, assigning, and managing tags.

Appearance

Appearance controls how RidgeNote looks. Change it from the Appearance control in the top bar or from Preferences -- both change the same setting, and either one applies immediately.

RidgeNote includes eight built-in themes:

  • Warm Light
  • Dark
  • Glacier
  • Granite
  • Alpine Mist
  • Blue Dusk
  • Midnight Ridge
  • Nightfall

Theme is saved to your account and applies across devices. The most recent successful theme change becomes the current theme everywhere.

Changing your theme on one device updates your account preference; other signed-in devices pick up the current theme the next time they load or refresh RidgeNote. RidgeNote does not offer separate theme choices per device.

The Sign In page always uses RidgeNote's fixed dark appearance; your saved theme applies once you sign in.

Account settings and Library Backups

Your Account and Preferences are separate from the contents of your note library.

A Library Backup contains your notes, folders, tags, and their relationships, but it does not replace your username, password, role, or personal Preferences.

This distinction is especially important when restoring a Library Backup into a different account.

See Library Backup and Restore for details.
